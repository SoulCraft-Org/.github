# Support

- **Bought a workflow?** support@soul-craft.com, with your order number. The workflow's handbook (linked from
  soul-craft.com/docs) answers most setup questions, and the "SoulCraft Status" workflow on your pod says what was installed.
- **ComfyUI Base** (public): open an issue on [SoulCraft-Org/ComfyUI-Base](https://github.com/SoulCraft-Org/ComfyUI-Base/issues).
