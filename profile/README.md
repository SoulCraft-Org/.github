## SoulCraft

Imagery and videos that delight, inspire, and educate. SoulCraft makes ComfyUI workflows that run on RunPod, for
creating pictures and movies of any character you can imagine, and sells them at [soul-craft.com](https://soul-craft.com).

Everything SoulCraft makes is safe for work, with no switch that turns that off.

- **[ComfyUI-Base](https://github.com/SoulCraft-Org/ComfyUI-Base)** (MIT) is the public toolchain every workflow builds on:
  ComfyUI at its newest release, the shared node packs, the model library layout, providers for RunPod, Verda and Crusoe,
  and survival across pod restarts.
- The workflows themselves are private repositories, one per product, sold with a licence key.
